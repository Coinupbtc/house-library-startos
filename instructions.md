## What you get

Open **Library** from the service page. It is a home page for the books stored on this server, and it works without the internet.

- One search box looks through every book at once. Titles are suggested as you type.
- Books are grouped on shelves: Wikipedia, health and medicine, home and repair, emergencies, learning and video, and reference.
- **Discover** shows a few videos and pictures picked at random from Crash Course, the Blender films, Wikivoyage, and Wikipedia when a picture edition is chosen. **Show others** picks again.
- **Surprise me** on a book opens a random page from it.

The default shelf has 19 books, about 84 GB in all. It includes English Wikipedia without pictures, WikEM, MDWiki (with pictures), MedlinePlus, iFixit repair guides, home improvement, cooking and gardening answers, emergency libraries, Crash Course videos, short animated films, Wikivoyage and the CIA World Factbook.

## Getting set up

1. Install House Library and start it.
2. Open **Library**. Books that are still downloading are greyed out. Each one becomes a link when it finishes. You do not need to refresh.
3. The **Books** status on the service page shows how many are ready and the progress of the current download.

Small books arrive within minutes. Wikipedia and Crash Course take longer.

## Choosing books

Run **Choose Books** from the service's actions. Tick the books you want and untick the ones you don't. Each line shows the size and whether the book has video.

- Wikipedia comes in one edition at a time: text only (49 GB), the million most-read articles with pictures (46 GB), or every article with pictures (119 GB). When you switch, the old edition stays readable until the new one has downloaded.
- New books download in the background.
- Books you untick are deleted once the other downloads finish. Ticking one again downloads it again.

## A note on space and backups

The books live on this server's data disk. The Choose Books result tells you how much the shelf takes up. If a book does not fit, the Books status says so.

A backup of this service keeps your book choices, not the books. After a restore, the service downloads them again.

## Who can read it

There is no account. Anyone who can open the address can read the books. Turn the address on or off from the service's interface list.
